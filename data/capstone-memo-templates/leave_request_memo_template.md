# Leave Request Memo Template

**Fictional company procedure, for training use only.**

Use this memo to request planned leave of five working days or more. For shorter leave, use the
self-service system instead — this memo is only needed once a request crosses the five-day threshold,
since it then requires cover planning.

## Template

To: [Line manager]
From: [Your name], [Team]
Date: [Date]
Subject: Leave request — [start date] to [end date]

I am requesting [N] working days of leave from [start date] to [end date] inclusive.

**Cover arrangements:** [name of colleague covering, or "no cover required" with a one-line reason]
**Handover:** [link to handover notes, or "not applicable"]
**Outstanding items before leave:** [list, or "none"]

## Approval process

1. Submit this memo to your line manager at least 10 working days before the start date. Requests
   during the annual maintenance outage (typically March) need 20 working days' notice instead.
2. The line manager checks cover and approves or requests changes within 3 working days.
3. Once approved, the requester updates the shift roster themselves — approval does not do this
   automatically.
4. Leave longer than 15 consecutive working days also needs sign-off from the department head, not
   just the line manager.

## Who approves what

| Leave length | Approver |
|---|---|
| 5–15 working days | Line manager |
| 16–30 working days | Line manager, then department head |
| Over 30 working days | Line manager, department head, then HR |
