# Procurement Approval Procedure

**Fictional company procedure, for training use only.**

This procedure covers purchasing spare parts, tools, and contracted services. It does not cover
capital projects — those follow the separate Capital Project Approval Procedure.

## Spend thresholds

| Value (AED) | Approval needed |
|---|---|
| Under 5,000 | Team lead |
| 5,000 – 25,000 | Team lead, then department head |
| 25,000 – 100,000 | Department head, then procurement committee |
| Over 100,000 | Procurement committee, then finance director |

Splitting a single purchase into smaller requests to stay under a threshold is not permitted and is
treated as a procedure violation if found during audit.

## Process

1. Raise a purchase request in the procurement system with a cost estimate and a one-line
   justification.
2. The system routes it automatically to the correct approver based on the threshold table above.
3. Approved requests go to the procurement team, who select a supplier from the approved vendor list
   where possible. A request for a supplier not on the approved list needs an extra sign-off from
   procurement before it proceeds, regardless of value.
4. Emergency purchases (equipment failure causing a safety or production stop) may be raised verbally
   to the department head for same-day approval, with the system request filed within 2 working days
   afterward.

## Common questions

- **Can a team lead approve their own team's purchase under 5,000 AED?** Yes, provided they are not
  also the requester. If the team lead is the requester, it goes to the department head instead.
- **What if a supplier isn't on the approved vendor list and the purchase is urgent?** The emergency
  purchase path still applies, but procurement must add or approve the supplier retroactively within
  5 working days.
