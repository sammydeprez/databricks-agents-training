# Change Request Memo Template

**Fictional company procedure, for training use only.**

Use this memo for any change to equipment, a system, or a written procedure. "Change" here means
anything that isn't a like-for-like replacement — if you're swapping a broken part for an identical
one, use a standard work order instead, not this memo.

## Template

To: Change Review Board
From: [Your name], [Team]
Date: [Date]
Subject: Change request — [short title]

**What is changing:** [one paragraph]
**Why:** [the problem this solves, or the requirement driving it]
**Affected systems or units:** [list]
**Safety classification impact:** [none / minor / major — see classification guide]
**Proposed implementation date:** [date]
**Rollback plan:** [what happens if this needs to be reversed]

## Approval process

1. Submit the memo to the Change Review Board at least 15 working days before the proposed
   implementation date.
2. Changes classified as "none" impact are reviewed by the board chair alone and typically approved
   within 5 working days.
3. Changes classified as "minor" or "major" go to the full board, which meets every second Tuesday.
   Major changes additionally require a written safety case, reviewed separately by the safety
   department before the board will vote.
4. No change may be implemented before written approval is received, regardless of urgency — an
   emergency change still needs a same-day verbal approval from the board chair, followed by this
   memo within 48 hours for the record.

## Who approves what

| Safety classification | Approver |
|---|---|
| None | Change Review Board chair |
| Minor | Full Change Review Board |
| Major | Full Change Review Board, plus a written safety case from the safety department |
