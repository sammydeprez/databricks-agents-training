# Materials and data

## Public documents used in Lab 4

All documents are published by their owners; do not redistribute the files outside the training workspace. Copies are bundled in this repository at `data/lab4-documents/` and copied into each participant's volume automatically by the **lab_data** setup notebook. The links below are where they came from, each checked and current, in case they're ever updated and need refreshing:

| Document | Owner | Pages | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| FANR-REG-12, Regulation for Emergency Preparedness and Response for Nuclear Facilities | FANR | 20 | [PDF](https://www.fanr.gov.ae/en/Documents/FANR-REG-12%20V1%20ENG%20for%20website.pdf) |
| FANR-REG-13, Regulation for the Safe Transport of Radioactive Material | FANR | 195 | [PDF](https://www.fanr.gov.ae/en/Documents/FANR-REG-13%20V1%20En.pdf) |
| FANR-REG-17, Regulation for the Certification of Operating Personnel at Nuclear Facilities | FANR | 14 | [PDF](https://www.fanr.gov.ae/en/Documents/FANR-REG17%20V.1%20EN%20-%20BOM%20Version%20Certified.PDF) |
| FANR-REG-30, Regulation on Technical Services Related to Radiation Safety | FANR | 8 | [PDF](https://www.fanr.gov.ae/en/Documents/FANR-REG-30%20V0%20for%20website%20English.pdf) |
| IAEA SSG-30, Safety Classification of Structures, Systems and Components in Nuclear Power Plants | IAEA | 44 | [PDF](https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/publications/PDF/Pub1639_web.pdf) |
| IAEA Nuclear Safety and Security Glossary, 2022 (Interim) Edition | IAEA | 248 | [PDF](https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/Publications/PDF/IAEA-NSS-GLOweb.pdf) |

FANR regulations carry a "Public" classification marking. IAEA publications are free to download and copyrighted; they are used here for training only. The glossary replaces the older "IAEA Safety Glossary" (2018 Edition), which its own cover page marks as superseded — use the link above, not the old one.

Skip anything marked "for official use only" (for example the regulatory guide for FANR-REG-08).

Limits: each file must be under 100 MB and 500 pages, in pdf, docx, pptx, txt or md.

## Capstone memo templates, used in Lab 4 (Knowledge Assistant)

Four short, fictional memo templates and procedures — leave requests, change requests, procurement
approval, and shift handover — written for this course, not copied from any real organisation. Bundled
in this repository at `data/capstone-memo-templates/` and copied into each participant's volume, in a
`memo_templates` subfolder, by the same **lab_data** setup notebook that copies the Lab 4 regulation
PDFs. Used by the **Memo and approval helper** capstone case (see [Capstone](capstone.md)).

## Synthetic datasets

Generated by `data/generate_synthetic_data.py` in this repository. Nothing in them is real.

| Table | Description |
|---|---|
| `work_orders` | 2,000 preventive and corrective work orders across four units, with status, priority, dates, asset and technician |
| `assets` | 120 assets (pumps, valves, transformers, sensors) per unit and system |
| `technicians` | 40 technicians with team, shift, and a masked phone number (see the data-masking exercise in Lab 2) |
| `it_tickets` | 500 IT service desk tickets, about 20 percent in Arabic |
| `incident_reports` | 60 short narrative reports for the extraction demo |
| `meeting_transcripts` | 10 short standup-style transcripts with action items, used in Lab 3 and the **Meeting assistant** capstone case |
| `monthly_kpis` | 12 months × 4 units of KPI figures (availability, planned maintenance completion, corrective work orders, safety incidents, budget variance), used in the **KPI and annual operating report** capstone case |

Generated fresh by the **lab_data** notebook, which every participant imports straight from GitHub and runs themselves at the start of the training (see [Before you start](setup.md)) — it creates the data directly in their own schema, nothing to download or upload by hand. The same notebook removes it again at the end (switch it to **Clean up my data**). See [Trainer notes](trainer-notes.md) for the one-time setup this depends on.
